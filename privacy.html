Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026. This policy explains how MLDY Labs handles information when you use this website and contact us.

Information we receive

When you use the contact form, we receive the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, selected topic, subject and message. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the contact form.

How we use information

We use contact information to respond to questions, provide support, investigate problems and communicate about the request you submitted. We do not sell personal information.

Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand general website usage, such as page views, visitor counts, device type and broad geographic information. We use this information to understand how the site is used and improve it.

Service providers

Our website is hosted using Vercel, including website analytics and public software-download storage. Contact-form email delivery is handled using Resend. These providers process information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy and security practices.

Software downloads

Our public Windows installers are delivered through MLDY Labs' Vercel-hosted object storage. Standard network and service logs may be generated when the website or download infrastructure is accessed.

Desktop applications

Some MLDY Labs applications are designed to perform their core work locally on your computer. Individual products may have additional privacy disclosures if they later use accounts, cloud features or other online services. Those disclosures will supplement this policy where applicable.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed for support, operational, legal and security purposes. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable safeguards and avoid placing private API credentials in public website code.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask a privacy question or request correction or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy as MLDY Labs and its services change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, use our contact form and select General Inquiry.

This website policy is intended to describe the current MLDY Labs website and download services. Product-specific terms or privacy notices may apply to future account-based or paid services.
